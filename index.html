About NAYA

Where movement, recovery, and culture meet.

Naya MVMNT is a bold, community-first wellness brand that mixes movement, recovery, and culture into one irresistible experience. We exist to reframe fitness away from guilt and aesthetics and toward joyful motion, better recovery, and social connection.

Purpose

Create joyful, high-energy spaces where people stay active and recover together.

Mission

Make movement social, recovery ritualized, and community the reason people show up.

Vision

A regional movement where fitness, culture, and celebration live side-by-side.

FAQ

Before you pull up.

Quick answers for first-timers, teams, and brands interested in joining the NAYA MVMNT experience.

Can beginners join?

Yes. NAYA sessions are built for different fitness levels, with energy, support, and community over ego.

What should I bring?

Bring a mat, towel, water bottle, comfortable trainers, and clothes you can move in.

Are events paid?

Some are free community jams, while premium workshops and special activations may be paid.

Do you do team building?

Yes. The full team-building package is KES 5,500 per person and includes movement, games, recovery, music, and facilitation.

How do I book NAYA?

Message NAYA on WhatsApp or Instagram with your event type, group size, preferred date, and venue.
