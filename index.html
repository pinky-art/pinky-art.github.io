About NAYA

Where movement, recovery, and culture meet.

Naya MVMNT is a bold, community-first wellness brand that mixes movement, recovery, and culture into one irresistible experience. We exist to reframe fitness away from guilt and aesthetics and toward joyful motion, better recovery, and social connection.

Purpose

Create joyful, high-energy spaces where people stay active and recover together.

Mission

Make movement social, recovery ritualized, and community the reason people show up.

Vision

A regional movement where fitness, culture, and celebration live side-by-side.
